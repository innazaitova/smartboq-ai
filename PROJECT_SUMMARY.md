## 💡 What It Does

### Input:
Construction drawings (PDF, DWG, DXF, images)

### Process:
1. **AI Analysis** - Claude Sonnet 4.5 identifies all structural elements
2. **Quantity Calculation** - Computes concrete, steel, formwork, masonry quantities
3. **Cost Estimation** - Applies regional unit rates and calculates costs
4. **BOQ Generation** - Produces professional Bill of Quantities in Excel/PDF

### Output:
Complete quantity takeoff + cost estimate in 30 minutes (vs 20 hours manual)
