# QuantiBot AI - Digital FTE for Quantity Surveying

**Transform 20 hours of manual takeoff work into 30 minutes of AI automation**

## 🎯 Product Overview

QuantiBot AI is an AI-powered Digital Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) that automates quantity surveying and cost estimation for construction projects. It analyzes construction drawings and generates detailed Bills of Quantities (BOQ) with minimal human intervention.

### Value Proposition
- **Cost Savings:** Replace $60K-90K/year junior QS with $24K-60K/year AI FTE
- **Time Savings:** 20-40 hours → 30 minutes per estimate
- **Accuracy:** 95%+ accuracy with continuous learning
- **24/7 Availability:** Process estimates anytime, anywhere

## 🏗️ What It Does

1. **Drawing Analysis:** Upload PDF/DWG construction drawings
2. **Element Detection:** AI identifies structural elements (beams, columns, slabs, walls, doors, windows)
3. **Quantity Calculation:** Extracts dimensions and calculates quantities automatically
4. **Cost Estimation:** Applies unit rates from comprehensive cost database
5. **BOQ Generation:** Produces detailed Bill of Quantities in Excel/PDF format
6. **Change Management:** Updates estimates instantly when designs change
