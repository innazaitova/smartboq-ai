## SALES PROCESS

### Stage 1: Lead Generation
**Goal:** Get contact info
**Tactics:**
- LinkedIn outreach
- Content downloads (BOQ template, cost calculator)
- Free trial signup
- Webinar registrations

---

### Stage 2: Qualification (BANT)
**Budget:** Can they afford $500-5,000/month?
**Authority:** Are we talking to decision maker?
**Need:** Do they do manual QS currently?
**Timeline:** When is their next project?

**Disqualify if:**
- Budget <$500/month
- No projects in next 90 days
- Not interested in automation

---

### Stage 3: Demo
**Format:** Zoom call (30 minutes)
**Agenda:**
1. Quick intro (2 min)
2. Understand their process (5 min)
3. Live demo with THEIR drawing (15 min)
4. Q&A (5 min)
5. Next steps (3 min)

**Key Points:**
- Show time savings (timer on screen)
- Show accuracy (compare to their manual)
- Show cost savings (ROI calculator)
- Handle objections in real-time

---

### Stage 4: Trial
**Offer:** Free first project OR 7-day trial
**Support:** Daily check-ins
**Goal:** Get them to process 3 projects
**Success Metric:** 95%+ accuracy vs their manual

---

### Stage 5: Close
**Pricing Options:**
- Monthly: Full price
- Quarterly: 10% discount
- Annual: 20% discount

**Objection Handling:**
- "Too expensive" → Show ROI calculator
- "Not accurate enough" → Show 95%+ accuracy data
- "We have someone" → Show time savings for existing team
- "Need to think about it" → Offer extended trial

**Close Techniques:**
- Urgency: "Launch pricing ends this month"
- Scarcity: "Only 10 spots left at this price"
- Risk reversal: "30-day money-back guarantee"

---

### Stage 6: Onboarding
**Week 1:**
- Kickoff call
- Upload first 3 projects
- Train on platform
- Set success metrics

**Week 2-4:**
- Weekly check-in
- Address accuracy issues
- Add custom unit rates
- Encourage referrals

## CUSTOMER SUCCESS METRICS

### Health Score Calculation:
- Usage: 40% (projects processed/month)
- Accuracy: 30% (95%+ accuracy maintained)
- Engagement: 20% (logins, feature adoption)
- Support: 10% (ticket volume, satisfaction)

**Red Flag Customers:**
- No usage in 14 days
- Multiple accuracy complaints
- Support tickets > 5/month
- Low engagement (<2 logins/week)

**Champion Customers:**
- >10 projects/month
- 95%+ accuracy
- High engagement
- Refer others

→ Ask champions for case studies, testimonials, referrals
